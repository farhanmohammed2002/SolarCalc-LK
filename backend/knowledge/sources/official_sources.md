## 1. Regulatory Documents & Government Gazettes
1. **Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL)**:
   - *Decision on Electricity Tariff Revision — Effective 18th January 2025*.
   - General Domestic Consumer Tariff Schedule (Decision Code: DOC/DEC/2025/01).
   - *Condition 3 regarding Fixed Charges for Net Metering / Net Accounting Consumers*: "The Fixed Charge for prosumers with rooftop solar shall be determined exclusively based on the net imported energy (kWh) recorded during the respective billing cycle."
2. **Ministry of Power and Energy, Sri Lanka**:
   - *National Energy Policy and Strategies of Sri Lanka* (Gazette Extraordinary No. 2135/61).
   - "Battle for Solar Energy" (Soorya Bala Sangramaya) Phase II Policy Directives.
3. **Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB)**:
   - *CEB Guide for Grid Interconnection of Embedded Generation (Rooftop Solar PV Systems)*.
   - Standard Agreement for Net Metering, Net Accounting, and Net Plus (Rev. 2024).
   - Feed-in Tariff gazette rate schedule: LKR 44.14/kWh ($\le 20\text{ kW}$), LKR 41.52/kWh ($20-100\text{ kW}$), LKR 38.90/kWh ($100-500\text{ kW}$).

## 2. Spatial Datasets & Solar Resource References
1. **World Bank Group & ESMAP**:
   - *Global Solar Atlas 2.0 (GSA)* — Solargis spatial radiation model for Sri Lanka (30 arc-sec resolution).
   - Data attributes: Photovoltaic Power Potential (PVOUT), Global Horizontal Irradiation (GHI), Direct Normal Irradiation (DNI), Optimum Tilt ($\beta_{\text{opt}}$).
2. **Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority (SLSEA)**:
   - *Solar Resource Assessment of Sri Lanka* (Wind and Solar Resource Atlas).
   - Technical guidelines on residential solar installation codes of practice.
