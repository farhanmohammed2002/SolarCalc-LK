# Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL) Domestic Electricity Tariff

## Regulatory Decision Baseline
- **Effective Date**: January 18, 2025
- **Issuing Authority**: Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL)
- **Document**: Decision Document on Revision of Electricity Tariffs – January 2025
- **Utility Scope**: Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB) and Lanka Electricity Company (Pvt) Ltd (LECO)

## Domestic Tariff Schedule (30-Day Billing Cycle)

### 1. Lifeline Consumption Category (Total Units $\le 60$ kWh/month)
Consumers consuming up to 60 units per month are billed under the Lifeline block rates:
- **Block 1 (0 – 30 kWh)**: LKR 4.00 per unit, Fixed Charge: LKR 75.00
- **Block 2 (31 – 60 kWh)**: LKR 6.00 per unit, Fixed Charge: LKR 200.00

### 2. General Domestic Category (Total Units $> 60$ kWh/month)
Consumers exceeding 60 units per month forfeit Lifeline eligibility and are billed cumulatively across standard volumetric tiers:
- **Block 1 (0 – 60 kWh)**: LKR 11.00 per kWh
- **Block 2 (61 – 90 kWh)**: LKR 14.00 per kWh
- **Block 3 (91 – 120 kWh)**: LKR 20.00 per kWh
- **Block 4 (121 – 180 kWh)**: LKR 33.00 per kWh
- **Block 5 (Above 180 kWh)**: LKR 52.00 per kWh

#### Monthly Fixed Charges for General Domestic:
- Net consumption 0 kWh: LKR 0.00
- Net consumption 1 – 30 kWh: LKR 75.00
- Net consumption 31 – 60 kWh: LKR 200.00
- Net consumption 61 – 90 kWh: LKR 400.00
- Net consumption 91 – 120 kWh: LKR 1,000.00
- Net consumption 121 – 180 kWh: LKR 1,500.00
- Net consumption $> 180$ kWh: LKR 2,000.00

## PUCSL Condition 3: Statutory Solar Prosumer Protection
Condition 3 of Annex 2 of the PUCSL Tariff Order explicitly mandates:
> *"Fixed charges for solar prosumers shall be based on net consumption."*

This statutory condition is critical for residential solar viability:
1. When a rooftop solar PV system offsets domestic electricity demand, the prosumer's fixed charge is assessed only on the **net imported energy** from the grid.
2. If the solar system generates energy equal to or greater than monthly consumption (net imported units = 0), the consumer pays **LKR 0.00 in fixed charges**, completely eliminating fixed utility overhead.
