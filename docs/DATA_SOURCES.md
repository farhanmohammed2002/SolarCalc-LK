# SolarCalc LK V1.0 — Authoritative Data Sources Register

| Category | Authority | Document / Dataset | Effective Date | Official Access | Purpose in SolarCalc LK |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Electricity Tariffs** | Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL) | Final Decision Document on Electricity Tariff Revision January 2025 | 2025-01-18 | pucsl.gov.lk | Domestic tiered rates (0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 91-120, 121-180, >180), fixed charges, and net-consumption billing for prosumers |
| **Rooftop Solar Schemes** | Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB) | Tariff Announcement for Rooftop Solar PV Systems | 2023-10-01 | ceb.lk | Export feed-in rate of 44.14 LKR/kWh for systems <=20 kW and 43.02 LKR/kWh for 20-100 kW under Net Accounting & Net Plus |
| **Solar Resource (GIS)** | World Bank Group / ESMAP / Solargis | Global Solar Atlas (GSA) v2.0 Sri Lanka Dataset | 2020 | globalsolaratlas.info | 30-arc-sec (~1km) raster ASCII grids of PVOUT (annual & monthly specific yields), GHI, and optimum tilt OPTA |
| **Secondary Solar Data** | NASA Langley Research Center | NASA POWER Agroclimatology API | 2024 | power.larc.nasa.gov | Validation benchmark for monthly daily insolation and ambient temperature cross-checking |
| **RTSPV Grid Guidelines** | Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL) | Guidelines on Rooftop Solar PV Installation for Solar Service Providers (Rev 1) | 2022-09-01 | pucsl.gov.lk | Interconnection standards, DC isolation, AC protection, inverter labeling, and utility approval requirements |
| **PV Modules** | Manufacturer Datasheets | EGing, SunPower, Qcells, BiMAX Official Datasheets | 2021-2024 | Manufacturer Portals | Electrical parameters (Pmp, Voc, Isc, Vmp, Imp, temp coefficients, dimensions) for sizing and string design |
| **String Inverters** | Manufacturer Datasheets | Sungrow, GoodWe, SOFAR, Solis Official Datasheets | 2020-2024 | Manufacturer Portals | AC power ratings, MPPT voltage ranges, maximum DC input current, and DC/AC sizing ratios |
